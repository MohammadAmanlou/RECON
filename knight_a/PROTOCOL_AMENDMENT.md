# Pre-evaluation amendment v1.1 — 24 November 2026

The original v1.0 primary contrast, question bank, family constraint, generators, split, grid, metrics and bootstrap remain unchanged. The previous smoke run was DEVELOPMENT only; no held-out outcomes were inspected. Add two prespecified secondary comparators, not replacements for the primary:

* `mixture_ig`: equal prior weights on the two development-fitted additive and conjunctive models. Maintain joint posterior over family and mastery state; select by mutual information about mastery Z, marginalizing family. No additional tuning or model-discovery claim. Secondary contrast pairwise IG minus mixture IG in mismatch; report descriptive paired CI, not confirmatory significance. This is a robustness hypothesis, not a new general Bayesian algorithm.
* `irt_cat`: scalar 2PL-style CAT with theta=(number mastered-3)/1.5, trained on the same fixed random eight-question development histories. Fit item discrimination in {0.5,1,1.5,2} and intercept in {-3,-2.75,...,3} by Bernoulli log likelihood, stable grid ties. Use empirical development ability prior and conditional skill frequencies with Beta(1,1) smoothing; select by Fisher information at posterior mean theta. It is a supervised synthetic ability proxy comparator, not a fitted human IRT scale. All methods use identical bank, eight distinct families, potential responses and independent world replication. Static pairwise BKT is a no-learning assessment adaptation, not full temporal BKT.

A joint-graph-discovery programme is OUT OF SCOPE. With the common response model P(Y|Z,q), I(G;Y|Z,D,q)=0; structure cannot be separately identified from one response conditional on known mastery. Model averaging is not graph recovery. Primary inference is restricted to the two specified conditional prior forms; general pairwise DAGs can encode conjunction. The bank measures conceptual recognition only, with unvalidated manual skill tags. Synthetic outcomes cannot demonstrate effectiveness on humans. Report negative or null outcomes, no post-test tuning. Sources: Wang (2013), doi:10.1177/0013164413498256; Templin & Bradshaw (2014), PMID 24478021; minimum expected risk CD-CAT, PMID 35528270; KNIGHT, PMLR 328:989–1024 (2026). Related work is a targeted initial review, not exhaustive novelty clearance.
